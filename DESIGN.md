# Studio design log

## Layout concept

The site uses an asymmetric editorial layout. Service businesses and freelancers can read the monthly price and responsibilities alongside a framed demonstration of the studio's design work. The home page pairs a broad type column with a small interactive gallery. Services uses a subscription specification, About presents a studio letter and working principles, and Contact places the project brief beside a routing directory.

## Visual system

Ink and cool paper frame the supplied Web Affairs identity. Midnight blue ties the architectural study to the corporate palette. Warm orange comes from the dot in the supplied wordmark and marks actions and active controls. Fine registration crosses and ruled captions connect the layouts. DM Sans sets the functional copy, Instrument Serif italic adds emphasis, and IBM Plex Mono labels the work.

Motion follows the drafting motif. A ruled curtain draws across page changes, captions rise into position, and a small crosshair responds to fine pointers. Keyboard controls and reduced-motion preferences have dedicated treatments.

## Content decisions

The architectural, print and typography studies demonstrate design choices. The business brief supplies no client testimonials, measured conversion results, founder identity, public website URL or contractual guarantees. The site draws proof from its interactive examples and the stated subscription terms. The contact form accepts briefs into a private studio database when served by the included handler. Static hosting offers a manual email handoff to the supplied enquiries address.
